# Changelog

What changed, newest first. Luna has not had a release yet, so everything is
under Unreleased; a release will take the entries since the last one.

## Unreleased

### 2026-10-01

- **Folders sit evenly in the sidebar** (§3.4b): a folder's row is 4 pt taller, so its icon has a little more room than a tab's favicon, and an open folder's plate keeps as much room over the folder as under its last tab, and as much beside its tabs as under them. The foot was twice the head.
- **Find on a page with ⌘F** (§18.1): a glass field in the page's top-right corner, with the count ("3 of 12"), Return and Shift-Return or ⌘G and ⇧⌘G to step, ⌘E to find the selection, and Escape to close. In Settings, ⌘F still searches the settings.
- **A Space's site answers are its own** (§5.9): Allow or Don't Allow for the camera, microphone, location, local network and pop-ups now counts only in the Space where you gave it, so a work account's camera does not carry over to a personal one. Answers given before are copied into every Space. Zoom, the ad blocker's exceptions and "never save passwords here" stay the same in every Space. A private window asks again.
- **A new extension runs in every Space** (§16.6) and can be switched off in any of them in Settings. A Space made later copies the others: an extension switched off everywhere stays off there too.
- **An extension card's warning keeps its words** (SETTINGS-SPEC §3.8): beside a card without a warning, the red line shrank to its triangle alone.
- **A new Luna starts with no colour** (§5.11): the first Space is named Personal in No Colour, and its dot wears no ring while it is the only Space.
- **Spaces no longer have a picture** (§5): the Picture row in Settings ▸ Spaces is gone.
- **Pages open sooner from the command bar** (§19): while the bar completes the address of a site you have been to, that page loads out of sight, and Return opens it already loaded (measured: BBC 513 → 5 ms, Apple 568 → 6 ms, GitHub 349 → 5 ms from Return to loaded). A guess you do not choose is dropped when the bar closes. Luna also keeps one web view ready for the next new tab, so a page it did not guess starts 20–35 ms sooner.
- **Fewer tabs reload when you go back to them** (§19.2): eight tabs stay awake instead of four, a tab you left sleeps after 30 minutes instead of 5, counted from when you left it rather than when you opened it, and a first memory warning keeps your three most recent tabs instead of only the one in front.
- **Websites can ask for the camera, microphone and location** (§17.8): a toast asks "Use the camera?" with the site's name, and Allow or Don't Allow is remembered for that site. The tab shows a camera or microphone while one is on, and clicking it turns them off. The site settings pop-out has a switch for each answer you have given. Before this, every request was refused without a word. Location needs macOS 27.
- **Only one Luna runs at a time** (§19.7): opening Luna again, or a second copy of it, brings the running one forward instead of starting a second window that could not open your data. If the data still cannot open, Luna says so in plain words instead of showing SQLite's error.
- **The Chrome Web Store's own button says "Add to Luna"** (docs/EXTENSIONS.md §4, UI-SPEC §5b): the store is shown a Chrome user agent so Google's "Add to Chrome" button stays live, and Luna relabels it and runs its own install when it is pressed. Where the button cannot be found — a Google redesign, the page not yet rendered — the toast offers Add as it did before. Either way the install prompt still asks.
- **A tab opened from a page opens right above it** (§6.5): in its folder if it has one, and to its right under the top bar. Closing it goes back to the page it came from, instead of the new tab going to the top of the list.
- **A native messaging host that answers quickly is heard** (§16): a reply that arrived before Luna was listening for it was dropped, and `sendNativeMessage` waited for good.
- **A docked inspector's top really is below the page bar now** (UI-SPEC §3.2b): WebKit reset the cover every time it docked or sized the inspector, so the fix of 2026-09-30 never held.

### 2026-09-30

- **The plan moved to GitHub issues**: every task in `TODO.md` is an issue on the [project board](https://github.com/users/ziadmyassine/projects/2), titled with its plan number, and the rest of the file became `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/DECISIONS.md`, `docs/RESEARCH.md` and this changelog. New: `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `SECURITY.md`, `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`, `docs/PRIVACY.md`, `docs/FAQ.md`, issue forms and Dependabot.
- **The app's code lives in `Luna/`**: `App`, `UI`, `Features`, `Design` and `Helpers` moved in, and the signing and CloudKit files into `Config/`. The unused formatter config is gone.
- **Add an extension from its Chrome Web Store page** (UI-SPEC §5b): a toast offers Add, which goes through the usual install prompt.
- **Extension cards drop their reach line** (SETTINGS-SPEC §3.8): only an extension that cannot work in Luna keeps a line there, in red.
- **A docked inspector starts below the page bar** (UI-SPEC §3.2b): docked beside the page, its toolbar was under the bar.
- **The Aa button only on reading pages** (UI-SPEC §3.2b): Reader and Markdown, where its settings change the page; not ordinary sites.
- **Extensions get native messaging, sign-in flows and the Chrome APIs WebKit lacks** (§16), and one Remove menu on every extension button.
- **One Escape ends hiding mode, fullscreen or not** (UI-SPEC §3.1): The picker heard Escape only while the page had focus; from the sidebar or a pop-out the press reached the window, which in fullscreen took it for the first of the two that leave. The window now ends hiding mode first.
- **Two Escapes to leave fullscreen** (UI-SPEC §3.1): One Escape nothing else took left fullscreen (`NSWindow.cancelOperation`). The first now shows a toast, "Press Esc again to exit full screen", and a second within 1.6 s leaves. Video fullscreen is unchanged.
- **Site settings keep their rows on a page that is not a site** (UI-SPEC §3.2a): On the new tab page, `about:blank` or a local file the pop-out was its heading alone, and glass that small is drawn lighter than every other pop-out (measured: full thickness from 107 pt tall). It now keeps the actions that need no site — Reader and Hide where they work, Share and Copy Link for a file, More Settings — and is at least 107 pt.
- **The bar finds a page's colour as the page finishes loading** (UI-SPEC §3.2b): The colour was sampled once at document end, before most sites paint their header, and not again until a scroll. It is asked again at `load`, 0.25, 1 and 2.5 s later, and when the document changes size.
- **Pictures beside a Markdown file load, and CI's render budget fits CI** (READING-PLAN): A Markdown page is a simulated load for its history entry, and a simulated load cannot read its folder, so `![](dot.png)` beside a README showed nothing (`MarkdownPageTests` failed on every push). Local pictures in the document's folder and below now go into the page as `data:` URLs, PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP up to 8 MB. The 5,000-line render test's limit goes from 100 to 200 ms of CPU time: debug builds measured 70–133 ms across machines, release 35 ms.
- **The bar keeps its colour while a page cannot be hit** (UI-SPEC §3.2b): Netflix switches hit testing off while it scrolls, so the sample found only `<html>` and the bar went grey for most of every scroll. A body missing from the hit test now means "cannot see": the bar keeps its last colour and the page is asked again every 250 ms until it can be hit.
- **The bar hears the page on the tab Luna opens on** (UI-SPEC §3.2b): At launch the selected tab's page is made after the bar looks for it, with no change in the session to make it look again, so the bar wore the document's colour (Netflix's grey) until the selection moved. It now hooks up on the tab's first state as well, and follows the tab's controller rather than only its id.
- **The bar reads shading over the page** (UI-SPEC §3.2b): See-through layers and top-to-bottom gradients are mixed over the colour behind them instead of skipped, so Netflix's bar is black like its menu (`5,5,5`, not the body's `20,20,20`). Nothing behind a JPEG or a video is mixed in. Checked against the screen on ten sites in a real `WKWebView`: getroosta.app is now right over its white horizon where it had said dark.
- **A folder goes with its last tab** (UI-SPEC §3.4b): Closed, dragged out, pinned or moved to another Space, the last tab takes its folder with it, agents' folders included; a saved folder waits until its dimmed row is let go too. Undo and ⌘⇧T bring the folder back. Only *New Folder* makes an empty one. The pointer left on a row the folder took with it no longer crashes the sidebar when it moves on.
- **An extension's tab no longer crashes Luna** (§16): An extension asking for a tab's web view in another Space got it, and WebKit stopped the app; it is now given only the web views of its own Space.
- **Pinned tabs in Safari's Favorites** (§31.10): Off by default. Safari's own iCloud sync carries them to the iPhone; Luna has a hidden Safari make the one edit that starts an upload.
- **The page bar has one state** (UI-SPEC §3.2b): The compact state that slid in as the page scrolled is gone: it was the bar's lag. The search bar stays full size, and the line left of the extensions button is gone.

### 2026-09-28

- **`⌘W` on a fullscreen video no longer quits Luna** (§19.2): Closing a tab while its page was fullscreen (a YouTube video, say) let go of the page while WebKit was still leaving fullscreen, and WebKit stopped the app. A closed tab's page is now kept until it is out of fullscreen and WebKit has answered, then let go a second later (`FullscreenTabCloseTests`).
- **Drop files and links on the window** (§6.6): A file or web link dropped anywhere but the page (sidebar, top bar, page bar, an empty window) opens as a new tab in that window. Dropped on the page, the page takes it if it has an upload area; otherwise WebKit opens it in that tab, as Safari does. Takes what Open With takes.
- **Reader, and hide anything for good** (§18.3): ⇧⌘R shows the article alone, in Luna's colours; Force Refresh moved to ⌥⌘R. ⇧⌘H turns the pointer into a picker: click a banner, sidebar or overlay and it is gone on that site for good, before the page draws on later visits. ⌘Z brings the last one back, Esc stops. Show Reader and Hide Something… are in the site settings pop-out. There is no list of hidden things: ⌘Z brings each one back, and once the undo list is empty — after a restart, say — it brings back the latest thing hidden on the page in front, one per press. While hiding, the pointer is a crosshair everywhere on the page and a toast says ⌘Z brings each one back. Kept per site in the database; a private window keeps its own in memory. Reader, hiding and ⇧⌘P Picture in Picture each say what they did in a page toast. ⌘Z first found nothing: a browser window answers `undo:` itself with a list of its own, so each window now uses its session's list (which also brings back ⌘Z for a closed tab), and the site settings pop-out gives the keyboard back when it closes.

### 2026-09-27

- **Bundle ID is `dev.novapps.luna`** (§31.1): Was `dk.novapps.luna`. The App ID and iCloud container were registered under `dev.novapps.luna`. Nothing was released, so there is no migration code.
- **Luna opens files, names them, and keeps what they saved in Dia**: A file opened in Luna shows its name in the search bar (it was blank). Luna claims PDFs, images, text, Markdown, JSON, CSV, code, SVG, XML, web archives, and MP4/MOV/WebM/MP3/AAC/WAV/FLAC/OGG — each measured as one WebKit shows (`LocalFileTypes`); a text file WebKit has no viewer for (YAML, TOML) shows as text instead of being copied to Downloads; File ▸ Open File… (⌘O). What a file page saved with `localStorage` in Dia, Arc or Chrome is copied into Luna the first time a file page opens, once (`FileStorageSeed`, `ChromiumLocalStorage` reads Chromium's LevelDB): the Roosta deck board opened with its Dia ticks. Luna was saving file pages' storage all along; the ticks were in Dia.

### 2026-09-24

- **The Downloads list a download opens does not block the page** (§15.3): A click beside it closes it and still reaches the page, so files can be downloaded one after another. A list opened with the button still catches the click.
- **The page runs under §3.2b's bar** (§30.27): Reverses "above the page, not over it": the web view fills the pane and WebKit is told the bar's height, so opening and closing the bar no longer resizes the page.
- **Clear is the only glass material** (§30.29): Removes the Clear / Opaque setting from Appearance.
- **Windows can wear macOS's corner** (§30.30): Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Match macOS corners: 16 pt instead of the default 25. The page's corners follow.
- **The sidebar's foot: the Space pill instead of the avatar** (§30.9, UI-SPEC §3.5): The Space's name, in its own pill, takes the Profile avatar's place at the bottom left, and the caption over the dots goes. The dots lose their glass pill.
- **A top-bar folder shuts in one movement** (UI-SPEC §4): Its plate morphs down to the name while the run slides, and the tabs fade in 0.10 s so their titles are gone before the plate passes them. It used to hold its full width until the fade was over and then snap, which read as lag.
- **`⌘W` with no tab open closes the window** (UI-SPEC §8): Close Tab was dimmed with nothing open, which swallowed the keystroke.
- **Settings › About, with updates from GitHub releases** (SETTINGS-SPEC §3.10): Icon, name and version, starting where the card starts (no inset); Check Now; install on its own (on by default); Install / Restart / Download for a newer release. Reads the repo's latest release once a day, checks the zip's SHA-256 and that it is a newer Luna, swaps it in, and Restart opens it. Only builds from the new `release.yml` workflow (run by pushing a `v…` tag) install; a Mac-built copy only links to the release. Not yet checked: the signer — that waits on Developer ID (§24.4).
- **Layout in Settings is a picture of each layout** (SETTINGS-SPEC §3.2): Two small drawn windows — tabs in a sidebar, tabs in a top bar — chosen by clicking, instead of a Sidebar · Top bar segment.
- **Double-click rename on the selected top-bar tab is instant** (UI-SPEC §4): The Command Bar the first click opened now closes at once and the name field is up on the second click. It used to wait for the bar to fold, and the page could take the keyboard back from the field.
- **Site settings stand centred on their button; icon tabs get them on right-click** (UI-SPEC §3.2a, §4): The pop-out is centred across the glyph instead of hanging from its left edge. A kept tile's right-click menu has *Site Settings…*, which selects the tab first if needed. The sliders and × on a tab now line up by their ink (they were 0.75 pt apart).
- **Every switch is the macOS Liquid Glass switch** (SETTINGS-SPEC §1.2): Settings and the site settings pop-out use `NSSwitch` (`SystemSwitch`) instead of Luna's hand-drawn 36 × 20 copy.
- **Site settings are a pop-out, and the top bar has them on the selected tab** (UI-SPEC §3.2a, §4): The site menu (`NSMenu`) is now a glass pop-out like History and Downloads, from the sidebar's pill, the page bar's and the top bar. Header says whether the connection is secure (green); Block Ads & Trackers, Automatic Picture-in-Picture and Local Network are switches; Share, Copy Link, Clear Cache, Clear Cookies and More Settings are rows. The selected top-bar tab shows the sliders glyph next to its close ×.
- **Top bar: History and Downloads open 5 pt off the capsule, as in the sidebar** (UI-SPEC §4): The gap was measured from the button, which sits 4 pt inside its capsule, so the list stood 9 pt off the glass.
- **Top bar: 50 pt tall, tabs 4 pt apart** (UI-SPEC §4): The same 7 pt above and below a tab (it was 7 and 9). Open tabs stand Dia's 4 pt apart instead of 8.
- **Top bar: a Space switch morphs instead of jumping** (UI-SPEC §4): The Space capsule changes width to the new name, and the buttons beside it and the tabs' edge move with it; the pinned-tabs plate changes width too. A click switch fades the old tabs out and the new ones in.
- **The top bar's Space name slides with a swipe** (UI-SPEC §4, §30.9): The name slides out with the tabs and in from the other side. No haptic: one was added and taken out the same day.
- **Luna opens HTML files, in the Luna that is running**: `Info.plist` claims HTML documents (as an alternate handler, not the default) and a file opened with Luna opens as a tab; before, it was dropped. A second copy of Luna started by macOS to open a file hands it to the running one and quits instead of opening a second window on the same database.
- **Every top bar tab is one width** (UI-SPEC §4): 172 pt, Dia's, measured; a longer title fades. Tabs were as wide as their titles, 120–180 pt. Folder headers still fit their names.
- **Top bar: Space swipes move the tabs; new tabs stay in view; the Command Bar stays inside the window** (UI-SPEC §4): The run of tabs slides with a Space swipe and the next Space's slides in; a new tab is followed as its title widens it; an opened Command Bar never rises past the window's top edge, from the tab or from either pill.
- **The Command Bar opens from a tab at once, and morphs** (UI-SPEC §4, §9.1): A click on the tab on screen opens it immediately instead of after the double-click wait; a second click folds it back and renames. The bar grows out of the tab's own frame and corner (width, place, height together, 0.26 s) and folds back into it; the sidebar and page-bar pills morph the same way.
- **Top bar: the page's top corners are rounded, and the bar has no glass of its own** (UI-SPEC §3.6, §4): Reverses §4's "flush full-bleed, no inset card": the page rounds its two top corners against the bar, as it does against the sidebar. The bar's own glass sheet (and the line along its bottom) is gone; it sits on the window's glass.
- **Top bar: back and forward before the tabs, the Space on the right** (UI-SPEC §4): The Space's name moves from the head of the pinned plate to its own capsule after History and Downloads; the profile button is gone.
- **No page bar under the top bar** (§30.27, UI-SPEC §4): Clicking the tab on screen edits its address in the Command Bar, which grows out of the tab the way it grows out of the sidebar's address bar; double-clicking a tab renames it.
- **A peeked sidebar stays out while its Downloads or History list is open** (§7.2): Until the list is closed, not until the pointer leaves the sidebar. The list never brings a hidden sidebar out by itself.
- **The hidden sidebar peeks the way Dia's does** (§7.2): 4 pt from the window's edge or anywhere past it, instead of 44 pt in; the screen's edge and the menu bar in fullscreen.
- **The Command Bar's search row leads when the query reads as a search** (§9.3): Changes §9.3's "adaptive matches rank above all": a learned page now sits one row below the search unless the query is the start of its address.
- **P3's tab switcher shows at most 10 tabs, only ones in use**: Two rows of five, centred on the window. Tabs past the tenth, unopened pinned tabs and Favorites are reached from the sidebar.
- **The Release build is stripped** (§24.10): The app is 14.5 MB instead of 24.4. Debug builds are unchanged; crash reports need the dSYM.
- **Extensions are in scope now**: Reverses §32's "extensions v2". §16 is no longer gated on a separate go-ahead.
- **Luna gets its own password manager**: Reverses §0.2 and §14's goal line. The Keychain bridge stays and becomes its storage or its fallback — P2.1 decides.
- **Top priorities: iCloud sync, Luna's own password manager, a tab switcher, extensions** — in that order (★): They come before the rest of M2–M4.
